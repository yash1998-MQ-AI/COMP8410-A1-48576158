# Data and provenance

Every image, video and scene used in the evaluation is listed here with its
source and licence. Nothing in this repository comes from earlier coursework.

## Privacy rules for this repository
- Raw phone photos of the home capture (Week 4) stay in `data/raw/` and are **not committed**.
  Publish only derived or downsampled frames after checking they show no address, mail,
  documents, screens, family photos or people.
- Strip EXIF/GPS before any photo is committed:
  `python tools/strip_exif.py data/raw data/public`
- Faces (Week 3): use only the student's own photos or synthetic faces, with consent.
  Record consent and source in the table below.
